ICMLSC 2027 (M4338) supplementary materials

Code, data, thermal logs and the pre-registration for "Internal Parallelism
Breaks Wall-Clock Budget Invariance in Restart-Fair Metaheuristic
Benchmarking". 15,600 measured conditions across five seed-blocked sessions
on one characterised platform.

analysis/reproduce_all.py regenerates every table and headline figure in the
paper and asserts the condition counts (15,600 -> 1,800 -> 1,488 -> 62 blocks).

Two things to know before loading the data, both documented in README.md:
data/voided/ holds a voided unpinned session that must not be analysed, and
tau.csv has a non-obvious lookup rule (PSO's tau is the ratio=0 row).
